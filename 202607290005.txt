Alice : Bob, have you ever thought about what it would be like to live on Mars? Bob : Alice, you always come up with the wildest questions! But yeah, sometimes. Mostly, I think about the commute. Alice : The commute? Seriously? I'm picturing us in a red dust buggy, exploring craters! Bob : And I'm picturing forgetting my wallet and having to turn back. That's a seven-month round trip! Alice : Details, details! Imagine the views! Sunsets would be epic – two suns sometimes! Bob : And dust storms that last for months. Your hair would be permanently crunchy. Alice : A small price to pay for being an inter-planetary pioneer! I'd totally grow potatoes, just like in _The Martian_. Bob : You'd probably name them all. "This is Spudnik, and this is Astropotato." Alice : Of course! And I'd build a giant greenhouse. You could be my chief Martian plumber. Bob : Me? I struggle with a leaky faucet on Earth! No, thanks. I'll stay here and send you care packages of fresh oxygen and proper coffee. Alice : But think of the bragging rights! "Yeah, I live on Mars. It's a bit dusty, but the neighbors are quiet." Bob : Quiet, yes, because there are no neighbors! What about pizza delivery? You can't live without pizza, Alice. Alice : We'll invent Martian pizza! With extra dehydrated cheese. It'll be a whole new culinary frontier. Bob : Right. And you'll probably try to teach the robotic rovers to fetch you a Martian soda. Alice : Only if they promise not to spill it in zero-G! It would be an adventure, Bob, a real adventure.
