Alice: Hey Bob, do you remember when we started learning English last year?
Bob: Oh, Alice! Yes, I do. My English was so bad, I thought "butterfly" was a flying piece of butter!
Alice: Haha! That is hilarious! And I thought "hot dog" meant a warm puppy!
Bob: We have learned so much since then. I can actually order food now without pointing at pictures and crying.
Alice: That is true! But yesterday, you asked the waiter for "human juice" instead of "orange juice."
Bob: Hey, English pronunciation is hard! At least the waiter laughed before he looked scared.
Alice: He looked terrified, Bob. But you are right, we are much better now.
Bob: Remember our first grammar lesson? I cried when the teacher explained the Present Perfect tense.
Alice: Me too. "I have eaten" sounded like a magic spell to make food disappear.
Bob: Now, look at us! We have traveled a long way on this language learning journey.
Alice: We really have. We can even watch movies with English subtitles now!
Bob: And we only need to pause the video ten times instead of fifty! That is real progress!
Alice: Next year, we will probably speak like native English speakers.
Bob: Absolutely! Until then, please promise you will never let me order "human juice" again.
Alice: Deal! Let's celebrate our progress with some real orange juice right now.
