Alice: Seriously? So, if I move across the country, my new doctor won't make me re-enact my entire medical history from birth?
Bob: Exactly! And not just locally. Many countries are moving towards national systems. Imagine, a doctor in, say, Norway, could potentially access your records if you fell ill there, with your consent, of course.
Alice: Norway? So my dodgy ear infection from last summer could be a global phenomenon? I'm not sure I'm ready for that kind of international fame.
Bob: It's all about continuity of care, Alice. It saves lives, prevents medication errors... and yes, saves trees!

Bob: Pretty much! And the US and UK are catching up, albeit with their own sets of challenges. It's a huge undertaking.
