Alice: Oh, Bob, my head really hurts today. And I feel a bit dizzy.
Bob: Alice! What's wrong? Are you sick? Maybe you have a serious illness!
Alice: Not serious, I hope! My throat is also a little sore. And I have a cough.
Bob: A cough? Oh no! Do you have a fever? Let me feel your forehead! (Reaches out)
Alice: Hey! No, I don't think so. I just feel tired. Maybe I need to see a doctor.
Bob: A doctor? For a little cough? Maybe it's just a common cold.
Alice: It feels worse than a common cold. My stomach also feels a bit upset.
Bob: Upset stomach? Oh, you definitely need medicine! Do you have any pain anywhere else?
Alice: Just a dull ache in my back. And my nose is running.
Bob: Running nose, headache, sore throat, cough, dizzy, upset stomach, back ache... Alice, you have ALL the symptoms!
Alice: Don't exaggerate, Bob! It's probably just the flu.
Bob: The flu? That can be serious! Did you get a flu shot?
Alice: No, I didn't this year. Maybe I should have.
Bob: You should call the doctor's office. Make an appointment.
Alice: Good idea. Thanks, Bob. I'll call them right away. And maybe get some rest.
Bob: Get well soon, Alice! I hope it's not the alien flu!
Alice: (Laughs) You're crazy, Bob! Just a human flu!
